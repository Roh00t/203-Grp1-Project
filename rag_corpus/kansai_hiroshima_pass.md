# Kansai-Hiroshima Area Pass

A regional alternative to the nationwide JR Pass: ¥17,000 for 5 consecutive days, covering Shinkansen between Shin-Osaka and Hiroshima plus JR lines around Kyoto, Osaka, Kobe, and Nara. For itineraries that stay within this region rather than starting in Tokyo, this pass is worth comparing against the nationwide pass and against individual tickets — the app's ROI logic should consider it as an alternative pass option, not just the nationwide one, when an itinerary's segments fall entirely within its coverage area.
