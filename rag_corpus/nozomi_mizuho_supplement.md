# Nozomi/Mizuho Supplement Rules

JR Pass holders may ride Nozomi (Tokaido/Sanyo Shinkansen) and Mizuho (Sanyo/Kyushu Shinkansen) trains, but only after buying a separate "Nozomi Mizuho Ticket" supplement — the base pass does not cover these services for free. The supplement cannot be purchased outside Japan; it's bought at a JR ticket counter, a ticket machine, or via smartEX once the pass is registered.

Confirmed flat supplement amount: ¥4,960 for each of these three pairs — Tokyo/Shinagawa↔Kyoto, Tokyo↔Shin-Osaka, and Shin-Osaka↔Hakata. The fee is distance-dependent overall (longer routes cost more), so this ¥4,960 figure should not be assumed to apply to other distances, including Shin-Osaka↔Hiroshima — that figure was not found in this research pass and needs sourcing before use.

If a pass holder rides Nozomi/Mizuho without the supplement ticket, they are charged the full normal fare on board rather than just the difference — always confirm train type before boarding.
