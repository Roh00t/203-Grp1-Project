# IC Cards and Non-JR Transit

The JR Pass covers JR-operated trains (including Shinkansen except Nozomi/Mizuho without supplement) but does not cover Tokyo Metro, Toei Subway, or private railways (e.g., Tokyu, Keio, Odakyu). An IC card (Suica or Pasmo) is needed for these — relevant because several tourist areas (e.g., parts of Shibuya, Asakusa's Tsukuba Express) are reached via non-JR lines the pass doesn't cover. The app's itinerary and ROI logic should not assume JR Pass coverage extends to every segment just because a segment is a "train."
