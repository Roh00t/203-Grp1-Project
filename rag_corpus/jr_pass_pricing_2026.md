# JR Pass Pricing (2026)

The nationwide Japan Rail Pass has been priced at ¥50,000 (7-day), ¥80,000 (14-day), and ¥100,000 (21-day) for Ordinary class since the October 2023 revision, with Green Car versions at ¥70,000 / ¥110,000 / ¥140,000. As of early September 2026 these remain the official prices for purchases made through JR's own site.

Since October 2023, JR Pass holders have also been permitted to ride Nozomi and Mizuho — previously excluded entirely — but only by paying a separate supplement (see nozomi_mizuho_supplement.md). This is a real, current caveat: a pass price alone does not tell you the full cost of using the fastest trains.

A reported overseas-agency price increase (to roughly ¥53,000 for the 7-day pass) takes effect October 1, 2026, for passes purchased through resellers outside Japan. Whether the official jrpass.com site price changes on the same date was not confirmed in this research pass — verify directly before stating it as fact in the report.
